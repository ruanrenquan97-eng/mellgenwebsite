cTDP Ingredient and Formulation Reference

This page describes the supplied cTDP liquid grade. It does not claim a penetration multiplier, treatment effect or suitability for delivering medicines. Experiments with other molecules or preparations cannot be applied to this grade without supporting evidence.

Supplier specification summary

The figures below describe the supplied ingredient grade, not a finished cosmetic. Supplier use levels are formulation-development references, not legal limits or a guarantee of safety or efficacy. Confirm the supplied version and batch COA; evaluate the final formula independently.

Ingredient composition (source declaration)1,2-HEXANEDIOL, DECAPEPTIDE-4, AQUA
AppearanceClear liquid with no characteristic odour
Raw-material pH2.0–5.0; Specification; not a formulation pH range
Composition / quality specificationDecapeptide-4: typical value 0.01% (supplier safety information)
Supplier use level (leave-on / rinse-off)1%–5%
Supplier-declared reporting code211755-00075-4448

Applies to the supplied cTDP liquid version, not the 1000 grade or energy-peptide blend. Adenosine triphosphate is not listed in this source composition.

Reporting codes identify supplier submissions; they are not regulatory approval of efficacy or safety.
