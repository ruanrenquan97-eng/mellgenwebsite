Ingredient Customisation and Development Enquiries

To discuss an ingredient project, specify the product type, target market, ingredient grade, sample needs and proposed formula. The scope, feasibility, testing plan and delivery terms must be confirmed for the individual project.

  1. Define the need and intended use
  2. Check composition, specification revisions and available documents
  3. Agree sample, compatibility and evaluation plans
  4. Confirm the actual manufacturer and applicable qualifications
  5. Agree contractual terms, quality specifications and delivery
  6. Check batch documentation and follow up on technical questions

Cosmetic-ingredient information on this site is not an offer of pharmaceutical, injectable, medical-device or food manufacturing services. Such uses require separate qualification and product-scope checks.

Ingredient references

  • cTDP peptide solution — cTDP peptide solution. Clear liquid with no characteristic odour. Raw-material pH 2.0–5.0. Supplier recommended use level: 1%–5%.
  • TFNpro Fibronectin — TFNpro Fibronectin. Colourless transparent liquid, odourless. Raw-material pH 7.0–9.5. Supplier recommended use level: 0.1%–5%.
  • Recombinant Type III Collagen — Recombinant Type III Collagen. Colourless transparent liquid, no characteristic odour. Supplier recommended use level: 0.1%–5%.
  • Peach Resin Extract Blend — Peach Resin Extract Blend. Brown liquid, characteristic odour. Supplier recommended use level: 0.5%–5%.
  • JELFIPRO Jellyfish Extract Blend — JELFIPRO Jellyfish Extract Blend. Pale-yellow to yellow colloidal liquid, slight characteristic marine odour. Supplier recommended use level: Leave-on 2%–8%; rinse-off 1%–3%.
  • MEGPEP TXOD Oxidoreductase Blend — MEGPEP TXOD Oxidoreductase Blend. Blue liquid, odourless. Raw-material pH 7.7–8.7. Supplier recommended use level: 1%–50%.
