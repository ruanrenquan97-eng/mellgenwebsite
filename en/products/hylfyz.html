Marine Brightening FactorProduct Introduction

Marine Brightening Factor is formulated by combining marine Schizochytrium ferment lysate with deep-sea alginate oligosaccharides. It intervenes across four distinct steps of melanin formation: pathway inhibition, tyrosinase suppression, melanosome transfer blocking, and accelerated turnover, unveiling luminous clarity.

Recommended Applications: Brightening essences, dark spot diminishing emulsions, revitalizing masks, and daytime sun defense boosters.
