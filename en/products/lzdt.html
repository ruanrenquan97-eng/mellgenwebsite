MEGCALM GLP
Ganoderma Polysaccharide

Ganoderma Polysaccharide (MEGCALM GLP) is a high-purity beta-glucan polysaccharide extracted from natural red Ganoderma lucidum. Naturally rich in bioactive polysaccharides, it delivers outstanding performance in deep skin hydration, antioxidant defense, barrier reinforcement, and gentle soothing care for sensitive skin. Utilizing gentle low-temperature enzymatic extraction and advanced fractionation purification, it fully preserves the natural bioactivity and structural purity of botanical polysaccharides.

Applications: Intensive hydration, soothing dry and tight skin, barrier recovery, and rejuvenating daily skincare formulations.
